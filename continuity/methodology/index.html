Publication standard

Methodology

A deliberately conservative standard for deciding what Ortema Continuity publishes and how uncertainty is shown.

Published: 21 August 2026Applies to the public Change Desk

1. Candidate detection is not publication

Automated tools and manual research may identify pricing edits, acquisitions, shutdown notices, terms changes, support-page updates or customer reports. A detected signal does not become a public claim until a person reviews the evidence.

2. Sources are classified

Primary sources include official vendor notices, pricing pages, support documentation, regulatory filings and published terms. Credible reporting can add context. Customer reports may indicate a pattern, but they do not establish that every account is affected in the same way.

3. Fact, report and inference are kept separate

Where a material statement cannot be verified with sufficient confidence, Ortema omits it or labels the uncertainty prominently. Ortema does not infer a vendor's intent from an acquisition, pricing change or complaint.

4. Status labels describe evidence

  • Watching: a relevant public signal is verified, but no separate customer-facing change has been established.
  • Confirmed change: a change is supported by a primary source or strong corroborating evidence.
  • Impact varies: the change is confirmed, but its effect depends on plan, usage, geography, contract or renewal.
  • Deadline confirmed: a primary source states an effective date or shutdown milestone.

5. Scope and unknowns remain visible

Incident pages state which product or plan is covered and what is not known from public information. Silence on a page is not a certification that a reader, vendor or account is unaffected.

6. Material changes are dated

Every incident page shows a visible verification date. A change that alters the meaning, evidence basis or affected scope should be recorded in the page's revision history.

7. Commercial relationships are disclosed

The current release contains no paid placements or affiliate rankings. Any future commercial relationship relevant to a page must be shown on that page, and payment must not be disguised as independent editorial judgment.

Publication boundaries

  • No vendor logins or API keys.
  • No customer invoices, contracts or exports.
  • No automatic stay, switch, cancel or renew recommendation.
  • No anonymous allegation presented as fact.
  • No ranking influenced by undisclosed payment.
  • No promise of complete or real-time coverage.
  • No public investment recommendation or market prediction.
