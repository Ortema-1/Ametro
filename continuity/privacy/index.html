Privacy and data use

The current public release is designed to work without analytics, user accounts, customer-file uploads, vendor logins or an email-alert database.

Last reviewed: 21 August 2026

Browser-only tools

The Harvest arithmetic calculator, checklist and evidence brief run in the visitor's browser. Values entered into those tools are not sent to Ortema and are not saved by the site.

RSS and calendar downloads

The RSS feed and calendar file can be used without creating an account or giving Ortema an email address. An RSS reader or calendar application may have its own data practices.

Who is responsible

ORTEMA LTD is responsible for the personal information it chooses to use through this website and related correspondence. Company details and the registered office appear below. Privacy questions can be sent through the main Ortema contact route.

Contacting Ortema

The contact form on the main Ortema website does not submit information to a web database. It opens a draft in the visitor's own email application. Nothing is sent until the visitor chooses to send that email.

If an email is sent, Ortema may use the sender's identity, contact details and message to respond, consider a requested project or relationship, keep necessary business records and comply with legal obligations. Where UK data-protection law applies, the basis will generally be Ortema's legitimate interests in handling genuine business correspondence, steps requested before a possible contract, or a legal obligation where relevant.

Retention and sharing

Correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, manage any resulting relationship, maintain necessary records or address legal claims. It may be processed by service providers used for email, hosting, security or professional administration, subject to their applicable safeguards. Ortema does not sell enquiry details.

Hosting and ordinary technical data

The infrastructure used to deliver and protect the website may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps and requested pages. Ortema has not added non-essential analytics, advertising pixels or behavioural tracking to this release.

External links

Incident pages link to vendor and documentary sources. Following an external link leaves Ortema's site, and the destination's privacy practices apply.

Data this service does not request

Ortema Continuity does not ask for customer invoices, contracts, employee records, client records, vendor passwords, API keys or software exports in this release.

Your rights and questions

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction, or to object to certain processing. Use the main Ortema contact route and identify the privacy or data-use question. Concerns may also be raised with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
