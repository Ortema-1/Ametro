From signal to evidence

The public desk is deliberately narrower than Ortema's internal monitoring.

What reaches the public site

  • 1 · DetectCandidate changes are found across vendor announcements, pricing pages, support documentation and credible reporting.
  • 2 · VerifyPrimary sources are preferred. Contradictory or account-specific claims remain explicitly uncertain.
  • 3 · PublishEach page records the change, affected scope, unknowns, sources and revision date.
  • 4 · InvestigateClient-side tools may help readers organise their own inputs without sending them to Ortema.

Publication boundary

Ortema Continuity is an information service, not a legal, financial, procurement or operational adviser. It does not certify that a company should stay, switch, cancel, renew or take no action.

Selected coverage: publication is intentionally limited to changes that can be checked carefully. Silence does not mean that a vendor or account is unaffected.
