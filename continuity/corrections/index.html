Corrections

If a material claim is wrong or incomplete, Ortema should correct the page promptly and record any change that alters its meaning or evidence basis.

Published: 21 August 2026

How to request a review

Use the contact route on the main Ortema website. Identify the exact page and statement, explain what you believe is wrong or incomplete, and include a primary source or other supporting evidence where possible.

How changes are recorded

Small spelling or formatting fixes do not need a public entry. A correction or update that changes the affected scope, the factual conclusion or the source basis should be recorded in the incident page's revision history with a date and concise explanation.

Vendor responses

A company mentioned by Ortema may submit relevant evidence for review. Providing evidence does not guarantee that Ortema will adopt a company's preferred wording; the aim is to correct factual errors and reflect material context fairly.

Urgent claims

Ortema may temporarily remove or qualify a disputed statement while evidence is reviewed. This does not imply that either side's position has been accepted.

Ortema Continuity

Corrections are part of the publication process.
