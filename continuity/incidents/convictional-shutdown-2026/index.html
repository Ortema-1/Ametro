Convictional: platform access scheduled to end 27 August 2026

Convictional says its platform is shutting down, with final access on 27 August 2026 and servers scheduled to go offline on 28 August. Its shutdown FAQ describes an export process and says remaining customer data will then be deleted.

Published: 21 August 2026Last verified: 21 August 2026Ortema Continuity deskShutdown · Data portability
Published deadline: this brief reports Convictional's own shutdown timetable. Ortema does not know any reader's account contents, contractual position or record-retention requirements.

What is verified

Convictional's official shutdown FAQ says platform services and access close permanently on 27 August 2026. It lists 28 August as the date servers go offline and says remaining customer data is securely deleted.

Published export position

The same FAQ says individual users can request an export from account settings. It says exports contain content the user can access, gives examples including goals, decisions, documents, posts, chats and meetings, and notes that a full organisation-wide export requires contacting Convictional.

What remains uncertain

Ortema has not tested the export process and does not know whether it covers every field or workflow a particular organisation may need. The official FAQ also says billing records should be retained separately from historical Stripe emails.
