Airtable: Bending Spoons has agreed to acquire the company

Bending Spoons announced a definitive agreement on 4 August 2026 to acquire Airtable. The transaction is expected to close later in 2026 subject to approvals. Until closing, the companies say they continue to operate independently.

Published: 21 August 2026Last verified: 21 August 2026Ortema Continuity deskOwnership · Monitoring only
No verified customer-facing incident: an acquisition agreement is a reason to monitor future changes, not evidence that pricing, terms, product access or data portability will change.

What is verified

Bending Spoons announced that it entered into an all-cash agreement to acquire Airtable. Its announcement says the deal values Airtable at an enterprise value of US$1.285 billion and is subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals.

Current operating position

The announcement says both companies will continue to operate independently until the transaction closes.

What is not established

Ortema has not verified any acquisition-related change to Airtable pricing, plan terms, exports, APIs or customer access. This page will not treat speculation or comparisons with other Bending Spoons acquisitions as evidence about Airtable.

Why this appears on the Change Desk

Ownership changes can be useful monitoring signals because later commercial or technical changes may follow. Under Ortema's methodology, however, a separate customer-facing change must be verified before the status is elevated.
