Thinkific: selected plan prices increased from 13 August 2026

Thinkific says prices increased by approximately 10% on Basic, Start, Grow, Legacy Pro and Fixed Growth plans. Timing depends on billing cycle, while Expand, existing non-profit customers and accounts with active discounts are listed as excluded.

Published: 21 August 2026Last verified: 21 August 2026Ortema Continuity deskPricing
Account-specific impact varies: this brief summarises Thinkific's published plan update. It does not establish what any individual account will be charged.

What is verified

Thinkific's support documentation says the 2026 change applies to Basic, Start, Grow, Legacy Pro and Fixed Growth plans for monthly and annual billing. The company describes the increase as approximately 10%.

Published timing

Thinkific says monthly customers move to the new price at their first billing cycle on or after 13 August 2026. Annual customers move at their next renewal on or after that date.

Published exclusions

Thinkific says the Expand plan is not changing and that existing non-profit customers and customers with active discounts are excluded from the increase.

What remains uncertain

Ortema does not know a reader's exact plan, discount, tax treatment, billing date or contractual terms. The vendor's own support page is the primary source for current account-level questions.
