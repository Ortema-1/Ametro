Vimeo On Demand: confirmed shutdown timeline

Vimeo says Vimeo On Demand will fully shut down on 20 November 2026. New uploads are disabled from 22 August, new purchases and subscriptions from 21 September, and viewer access ends with the service.

Published: 21 August 2026 Last verified: 21 August 2026 Ortema Continuity desk Primary-source basis
Add official dates to calendar Download timeline graphic
Scope: this brief concerns Vimeo On Demand, not every Vimeo product or every video stored in a Vimeo account.

What Vimeo has confirmed

Vimeo's official shutdown notice says Vimeo On Demand is being wound down in stages. The published milestones are:

DatePublished changeWho it concerns
22 August 2026New uploads to Vimeo On Demand are disabled.Sellers
21 September 2026New purchases and subscriptions are disabled. Active subscriptions do not renew after this date.Sellers and viewers
21 October 2026Vimeo says final eligible seller payouts are expected to be completed.Sellers
20 November 2026VOD storefronts go offline and viewer access to purchased or rented VOD content ends.Sellers and viewers

What Vimeo says about seller data

Vimeo says the underlying videos in a seller's Vimeo account are not deleted merely because Vimeo On Demand closes. It recommends exporting any needed sales data from the analytics dashboard before 20 November 2026.

Vimeo also documents a CSV export of email addresses for viewers who explicitly opted in to updates from the seller's VOD page. Vimeo says it does not provide a full buyer list for privacy reasons.

What Vimeo says about viewers

Viewers can continue using purchased and rented VOD content until 20 November 2026, subject to ordinary rental expiry. Vimeo says downloads depend on whether the creator enabled them for the title. It also says purchase entitlements and watch history cannot be transferred to another platform.

Vimeo's published OTT route

Vimeo presents Vimeo OTT as one route for creators who want to continue selling video. Its seller FAQ says existing Vimeo videos can be imported into an OTT site without re-uploading. The same FAQ separately describes exporting opted-in audience emails and asking Vimeo Support to notify buyers on a seller's behalf. Those features are not a transfer of viewer purchase entitlements.

What remains account-specific or unknown

  • Which titles in a particular viewer's library have downloads enabled.
  • Which records a particular seller needs for accounting, tax, rights management or customer support.
  • Whether any replacement service reproduces a seller's current checkout, rental, subscription, regional-rights or audience workflow.
  • How a seller should communicate with buyers who did not opt in to direct updates.
  • Whether an individual account has refund, contract or support questions that require Vimeo's response.

Questions a seller can verify

These are investigation prompts, not a recommendation to move to any particular service:

  • Which VOD titles, sales reports and analytics records need to remain available after shutdown?
  • Which viewers are included in Vimeo's documented opted-in audience export?
  • Which public links, embeds and customer communications currently point to VOD storefronts?
  • Which parts of the catalogue, audience relationship and transaction history can a prospective destination actually accept?
  • What would need to be tested before any public replacement is announced?
