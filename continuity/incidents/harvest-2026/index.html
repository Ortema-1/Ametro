Harvest: ownership and current pricing structure

Harvest was acquired by Bending Spoons in July 2025. Harvest’s current public pricing lists base per-seat rates for Teams and Enterprise and states that additional invoices, projects, clients and tasks can be billed based on usage. The financial effect on any particular customer depends on its own plan, usage and renewal terms.

What is verified

Bending Spoons’ 2026 prospectus states that it acquired Harvest in July 2025. Harvest’s current pricing page lists Teams from US$9 per seat/month when billed annually and Enterprise from US$14 per seat/month when billed annually. Harvest also states that, as a team grows, additional invoices, projects, clients and tasks are billed according to usage.

What remains uncertain

Public pricing does not reveal any individual customer’s contract, legacy-plan treatment, account usage or renewal quote. Customer reports can provide useful signals but are not treated by Ortema as proof of what another account will experience.

Published data portability

Harvest documents exports for time, expenses, invoices, estimates, projects, people, clients and other reports. Administrators can export all company time as CSV and all invoices as a ZIP containing PDF invoices. Availability can depend on plan and permissions.

Example documented migration route: Toggl Track

Toggl Track currently documents a Harvest importer for clients, projects, active tasks, billing rates and up to 12 months of time entries. Toggl states that invoices, expenses, project codes/notes, custom fields, archived/deleted data and several other structures are not imported. This is an example of published compatibility, not an Ortema recommendation.
