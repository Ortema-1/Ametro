About ORTEMA

Small enough to move quickly.

ORTEMA LTD is an independent technology company based in the United Kingdom. We build and test practical technology across software, artificial intelligence, robotics and related emerging fields.

We do not force every problem into the same technology stack. The aim is simple: identify useful opportunities, make working things quickly, learn from real use, and develop the ideas that earn the right to go further.
